Mental Health
Meets Neuroscience.

The brain, behaviour and mental health are deeply interconnected. Some psychiatric symptoms occur alongside neurological or medical conditions and require a broader neuro-behavioural perspective.

Headache & Migraine

Recurrent headaches and migraines can interact with sleep, stress, mood and other neurological factors. Appropriate clinical assessment helps guide management.
