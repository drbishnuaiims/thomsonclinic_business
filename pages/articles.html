Featured insight

When is a professional assessment useful?

Persistent, distressing or changing symptoms can be worth discussing with a qualified clinician. Assessment helps put symptoms in their appropriate context.

Mental health

Sleep, stress and emotional wellbeing

These areas often interact; paying attention to patterns can be a helpful first step.

Neuroscience

Brain, behaviour and mental health

A broad perspective can be helpful when symptoms overlap with neurological conditions.

Important

Screening is not diagnosis

Screening instruments can guide discussion but cannot replace a clinical assessment.
