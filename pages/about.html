About Thomson Clinic

Thomson Clinic is a Mental Health & Neuroscience eClinic focused on confidential, evidence-based and individualised care.

Our approach

Clear, considered, connected.

Mental health, behaviour and brain health can be closely connected. Our approach makes room for that complexity while keeping the next step clear and respectful.

Online care can make a clinical conversation more accessible, while appropriate assessment remains central to understanding individual concerns.
